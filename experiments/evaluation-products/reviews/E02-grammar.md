Reviewed `cases/E02-training/input.md` and `evaluator.md` against the current source and public conformance at support HEAD `837290a83911e160f27835f246a5bd7df21107b1`. ## Assessment

The 42 requirements form a coherent product and use supported structural constructs for the production policy, typed provenance, exact templates, dates, relations, roles, and lookup/recovery contracts. No requirement needs a general join, snapshot, event constant, conditional event field producer, cross-record date expression, or runtime delivery engine. The evaluator correctly separates human semantic assessment from compile success. Two material compiler limits must remain explicit: the legacy global lifecycle check does not understand external supply/lookup as producers, and reachable conditional Create/Skip prevents workflow completion availability proof. Neither warrants deleting product requirements or inventing extra creation behavior.
